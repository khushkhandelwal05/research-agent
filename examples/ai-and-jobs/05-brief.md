## Limitations and method

- Topic statements were written from the topic alone, before any source was read; a source that does not take a position on a statement counts neither for nor against it.
- Claims that fit none of the statements are listed as single-source and were not compared across sources.
- Claims were extracted and matched to statements by a language model. Every quote was checked against the source text, but whether a quote supports or opposes a statement is the model's judgment: read the quotes before relying on a row.
- A source's side on a statement needs at least a 2:1 majority of its claims; otherwise it counts as mixed.
